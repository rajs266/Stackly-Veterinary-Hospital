Doctor Tips

Clinical Guidance From Our Doctors

Practical health advice for Salem pet families direct from our medical room.

Summer Heatstroke Awareness

“Salem temperatures during summer can cause rapid dehydration in short-muzzled breeds like Pugs and Shih Tzus. Provide fresh water and avoid walks between 11 AM and 4 PM.” — Dr. Rajesh Kumar

Early Dental Disease Signs

“Bad breath in cats and dogs is not normal — it indicates bacterial tartar buildup. Annual ultrasonic scaling prevents tooth extractions later in life.” — Dr. Priya Sharma

Monsoon Skin Allergies

“Humidity causes paw licking and ear infections in Indie dogs and Golden Retrievers. Cytology skin scrapes identify fungal spores early for fast relief.” — Dr. Anitha Ramesh
