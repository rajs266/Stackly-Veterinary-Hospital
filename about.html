Our Story & Purpose

Dedicated To Excellence In Pet Health Since 2011

Founded at MMR Complex, Chinna Thirupathi in Salem, Stackly Veterinary Hospital was established with a singular vision: to provide tertiary-level medical care, emergency trauma intervention, and preventive medicine for dogs, cats, and small animals.

Our multi-specialty facility houses state-of-the-art sterile surgical suites, digital diagnostic imaging, blood pathology laboratories, and comfortable recovery wards.

Hospital Milestones

Our 15-Year Journey Of Healing

Key milestones that transformed Stackly into Salem's leading multi-specialty pet hospital.

2011

Foundation in Salem

Started as a single consult room at MMR Complex, Chinna Thirupathi, focusing on gentle canine and feline outpatient care.

2016

24/7 Surgical ICU Wing

Expanded to include a sterile soft-tissue operating theatre, dedicated recovery kennels, and round-the-clock emergency triage.

2021

Advanced Diagnostic Imaging Lab

Introduced non-invasive Doppler ultrasound scanning, digital radiography, and ultrasonic dental prophylaxis scaling units.

2026

Digital Pet Pass System

Over 8,500 successful surgeries completed with instant cloud patient portals for vaccine records and prescription tracking.
