Preparation Guide

Preparing Your Pet For A Visit

Follow these simple steps before arriving at our Salem clinic for a smooth consult.

Fasting Before Surgery

Withhold food 8-10 hours prior to anesthesia for surgical or dental procedures. Water is permitted up to 2 hours before.

Bring Past Medical History

Bring previous vaccination cards, blood test reports, and current medication packets for our doctors to review.

Leash & Carrier Safety

Ensure dogs are on secure leashes and cats/small pets are inside ventilated travel carriers upon arrival at MMR Complex.
